Pātāl — engineering harness

Not the product. This is a disposable window onto the Rust engine — the only thing in this repo that runs on a Windows machine, and the only way to look at a seam allowance with your eyes instead of reading it out of a test assertion. See ADR-005.

An industrial cutter works to about 0.4mm. Anything finer is spent on the screen, not on the garment.

Drag it negative to inset. Push it far enough and the engine refuses — that refusal is the point, not a bug.
